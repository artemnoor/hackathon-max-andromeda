# Roadmap

## MVP / Private Alpha

Реализовано: source-backed BMSTU и HSE adapters, canonical ingestion, university-scoped identity, curriculum/admissions, Admission Fit, Content Fit, DecisionContext, persistent shortlist, explicit final choice, comparison, anonymous/account persistence, source gaps, provenance, analytics и operational ingestion audit.

## Post-MVP

- Source-backed education-policy pilot: bounded approved-source polling,
  reviewed claims, exact-hash policy approval, deterministic effective rules,
  provenance and assistant queries are implemented behind rollout/operations
  gates. Next work is source-steward and reviewer provisioning, a verified
  official document corpus, domain-owner impact bridges, PostgreSQL performance
  evidence and controlled cohort rollout. Broad news monitoring/open crawl and
  unreviewed Jev policy operations are out of scope.
- Career Fit на source-backed graduate outcomes и skills → occupation mapping.
- Валидированный персональный Workload Readiness.
- Дополнительные университеты через adapters.
- Более глубокая campus/map поддержка.
- Карьерные outcomes и уведомления.
- Advanced analytics и внешнее безопасное sharing.
